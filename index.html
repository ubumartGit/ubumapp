What we are

A one-person studio, run like it isn't.

Ubumapp is an independent development studio. There's no venture funding, no growth team, no dashboard full of engagement metrics — just one developer building freemium mobile apps that are meant to be used once and trusted forever.

Every app under the Ubumapp name follows the same rule: do one thing well, ask for nothing you don't need, and never make the free tier feel like a trap.
